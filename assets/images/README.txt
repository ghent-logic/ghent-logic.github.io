BACKGROUND PHOTOGRAPH
---------------------
Put a photograph of Ghent here as ghent.jpg and it appears behind the heading
of every page, edge to edge, under a pale wash. No other change is needed.

  - About 2000 pixels wide is plenty; keep the file under roughly 400 KB, or
    every page will be slow to load. Save as JPEG at around 70% quality.
  - A wide, calm picture works best. The headline sits over the left half, so
    avoid anything busy or bright there.
  - Use a picture you took, or one that is licensed for it: Wikimedia Commons
    (credit required) or Unsplash (no credit required). If a credit is needed,
    put it in photoCredit in content.js and it appears in the footer.
  - To adjust it afterwards, --photo-wash and --photo-position sit at the top
    of assets/style.css: the wash controls how much shows through, the position
    which part of the picture is framed.
